# 🕊️ Psycheflux Oracle - The Living Mirror of Light

*A Two-Phase Oracle Reflection Ritual in p5.js – Opaline Dreamshell Edition*

A nacreous ritual space for symbolic emergence, dream-truths, and soft intuitive reflection.

## ✧ About

The Psycheflux Oracle is an interactive web experience that combines generative art with symbolic interpretation. Through two distinct phases, users journey from witnessing a living, morphing inkblot to capturing and interpreting their personal symbolic visions.

### Phase 1: The Living Mirror of Light

An eternally morphing inkblot generated using Perlin noise and flow fields:
- **Vertical symmetry** - Like a true Rorschach test
- **Organic complexity** - Multiple blob segments with tentacles, lobes, and negative space
- **Opalescent colors** - Soft seafoam green (#ADDFD6) and lilac (#EADAEA)
- **Glowing aura** - Subtle pearl-white shimmer around each form
- **Spirit Pearls** - Four pulsing orbs in the corners, breathing like tides
- **Freeze gesture** - Hold click/touch for 2 seconds to capture your vision

### Phase 2: The Reflective Tides Within

A space for interpretation and oracle wisdom:
- **Reflection prompt** - "What do you see beneath the surface?"
- **Symbolic readings** - 30+ archetypal symbols (shell, pearl, wave, spiral, swan, etc.)
- **Oracle messages** - Poetic interpretations for each symbol
- **Save your vision** - Export both the frozen inkblot image and your reflection data

## 🌊 Features

- **Fully responsive** - Works on desktop and mobile
- **Touch-friendly** - Supports both mouse and touch interactions
- **Generative art** - Each inkblot is unique and ever-changing
- **Symbolic wisdom** - Deep archetypal readings from 30+ symbols
- **Export capability** - Save both visual and textual records

## 🎨 Symbolism Dictionary

The oracle recognizes 30+ symbols including:

**Living Beings**: jellyfish, swan, bird, butterfly, serpent, tree, flower, lotus

**Transformative Symbols**: spiral, wing, root, crystal, feather, eye

**Spiritual Concepts**: mirror, veil, vessel, labyrinth, bridge, cave

Each symbol carries its own oracle message about transformation, wisdom, and inner truth.

## 🚀 Usage

1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser
2. Watch the living inkblot morph and flow
3. When a shape speaks to you, **hold your click/touch for 2 seconds**
4. The inkblot will freeze and the reflection interface will appear
5. Describe what you see in the text input
6. Click "Receive Oracle" to get your symbolic reading
7. Save your vision as PNG + JSON for future reflection
8. Click "Return to the Mirror" to begin again with a new inkblot

## 🛠️ Technical Details

- **Framework**: p5.js (v1.7.0)
- **Fonts**: Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts)
- **Animation**: Perlin noise for organic motion
- **Rendering**: HTML5 Canvas with shadow effects
- **Responsive**: Adapts to all screen sizes
